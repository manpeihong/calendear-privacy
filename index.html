CalenDear — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

CalenDear ("the App") is a shared calendar, chore, and grocery organizer for families. We built it privacy-first: your family's information stays with you, and the small amount of data that ever leaves your device is stripped of names before it goes. This policy explains what data the App handles, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Your Family's Data Stays on Your Devices and in Your iCloud

Everything you create in CalenDear — family member profiles, calendar events, to-dos and chores, grocery lists, rewards and stars — is stored on your device and synced across your family's devices through your own Apple iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit).

  • This data lives in your private iCloud storage. We (the developer) cannot see it and never receive a copy of it.
  • When you invite family members, their devices access this data through Apple's secure family-sharing mechanism — again, without it passing through our servers.
  • Its handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

2. On-Device Intelligence

Some features use Apple's on-device AI and speech recognition — including natural-language "quick add," grocery aisle sorting, and voice input. These run entirely on your device. The text and audio involved are processed locally and are not sent to us or anyone else.

3. Smart Insights (CalenDear+)

If you subscribe to CalenDear+, the App can generate weekly digests and chore-fairness check-ins. To produce these, the App sends a short pseudonymized summary of your upcoming schedule and chore counts to our server. Before anything leaves your device:

  • Every family member's real name is replaced with a neutral placeholder (e.g. "Parent 1," "Child 2"). The real names are restored only on your device after the insight comes back.
  • The summary is used solely to generate that one insight. It is not stored, logged, or retained on our server after the reply is delivered.
  • To generate the text, our server forwards the pseudonymized summary to a third-party AI provider (currently Google Gemini). Per Google's API terms, inputs sent through the API are not used to train their models.

4. External Calendar Overlay (CalenDear+)

If you enable the optional external-calendar feature, CalenDear reads events from the calendars already on your device (Apple Calendar, and Google or Outlook if you've added those accounts in iOS Settings). This access is read-only — CalenDear never edits or adds anything to those calendars. What it reads is displayed on your device and, if you choose to share it with your family, synced through your own iCloud as described in Section 1. It is never sent to our servers.

5. Subscriptions

CalenDear+ subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store. Apple handles all payment information; we never see your payment details. To confirm an active subscription, the App sends Apple's cryptographically signed subscription receipt to our server for verification. This receipt confirms subscription status only and contains no personal information about you or your family.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs in the App.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or track you across other apps or websites.

7. Children's Privacy

CalenDear is a family-organizer app set up and controlled by a parent or guardian. Children's names and profiles are created by the adult running the family, are stored in that family's private iCloud (Section 1), and are never sent to our servers. Insights that reference the family use the pseudonymized placeholders described in Section 3, so a child's real name never leaves the device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children directly.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your family's data lives in your own iCloud, you control it. You can delete any item, or use "Erase All Data" in the App's Settings to remove everything. Deleting the App and removing its data from iCloud removes it entirely. We hold no server-side copy to delete.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
